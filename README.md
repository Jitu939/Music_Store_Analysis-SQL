# Music_Store_Analysis-SQL

## Project Overview
This project performs an in-depth analysis of a digital music store's database to uncover business insights regarding sales, customer behavior, and inventory. By executing complex SQL queries, the analysis helps the store understand its best-performing markets, top-spending customers, and the most popular music genres across different countries.
## Tools
SQL Workbench Concepts Used: CTEs (Common Table Expressions), Window Functions (ROW_NUMBER), Joins, Aggregations, and Subqueries.
## Key Business Questions Answered
Senior Employee: Identified the senior-most employee based on job title to understand organizational hierarchy.
Top Markets: Determined which countries have the highest number of invoices to identify primary sales regions.
High-Value Sales: Identified the top 3 values of total invoices.
Genre Targeting: Retrieved a list of all "Rock" music listeners (Email, First Name, Last Name) for targeted marketing campaigns.
Top Artists: Identified the top 10 rock bands based on their total track count in the dataset.
Customer Value: Calculated the total amount spent by each customer on specific artists.
Popular Genres by Country: Used CTEs to determine the most popular music genre for each country based on the number of purchases.
Top Customer per Country: Identified the highest-spending customer in each country, providing a detailed breakdown of their total expenditure.
